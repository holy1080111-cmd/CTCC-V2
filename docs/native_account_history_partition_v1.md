## Pure storage records

`derive_native_member_registration_v1` binds the exact existing canonical B3 document, event/anchor/receipt hashes and its original five-field B1 source reference to a native linkage. The linkage preserves the originally acquired latest source reference, original invocation and DB0046 custody identities, bundle/proof/policy/manifest hashes, original raw/companion inventories and distinct private root locators (exact path, device and inode), and original UTC/monotonic anchors. Its inventory uses the native issuer's original `[size, SHA256]` representation. Private root locators belong to protected custody evidence and must be redacted from public reports. It never changes a reference, generates a new timestamp or treats older sources within a bundle as newly attested.

The function verifies structural consistency. Its portable inputs and `HistoryPartitionRecordV1` result are inert. They cannot prove actual ownership, B1 replay, TLS, DB custody, credential continuity or native clocks. The future held producer must supply those facts from its original private binding, retain full original files, and replay them before and after every await. No caller-provided record, hash or boolean is an alternative authority.

`build_partition_checkpoint_v1` seals exactly32 consecutive global B3 sequences. Partition1 is1..32; partition2 is33..64. A partial suffix cannot be sealed. Every record retains the original complete canonical B3 document and either its exact registration record or explicit `None`. A missing historical native registration remains a gap; a new proof cannot fill it. The checkpoint binds genesis, predecessor checkpoint and terminal B3 anchors, ordered membership, cumulative source/coverage/unresolved-finding counts, all original locators, and both per-partition and cumulative missing-registration evidence. Existing findings and proof-budget overflow are preserved.

`replay_partition_checkpoints_v1` starts at sequence1, replays every provided partition and original B3 document, recomputes the immutable chain, and requires the expected final checkpoint hash and B3 head. It rejects missing first/middle members, changed scope/policy/anchors/counts, duplicate captures or native invocations across partitions, malformed/noncanonical JSON, nested duplicate keys, and modified authority fields. There is no global32-partition limit. The cumulative64MiB proof-byte and8MiB per-record budgets are checked without truncation or per-partition resetting. This is a bounded structural replay, not an unlimited-lifetime promise.

Every storage record preserves `source_authenticity_verified=False`, `account_complete=False`, `execution_authority=False`, `account_revision=None`, `clear_stop_permission=False`, `arm_permission=False`. Funding accrual, account-wide finality, current history tail, loss-streak seed and historical native HWM remain `None`.

It needs two immutable tables (original source-member linkage and full partition checkpoints), original B3/B1/custody constraints, exact UID serialization and head CAS, function-only restricted-role writes, main/custody role separation, complete catalog/ACL verification, commit plus distinct-session readback, and preservation of committed rows on late failure or unknown commit. Migration0050 and all current-head CI/migration readers must be added together. Historical0048/0049 source stays unchanged.

The native adapter must accept only the original keyed history binding, prove the actual latest acquired member, replay B1/B3/DB0046 and raw/companion inventories, maintain original source/function/factory pins and both original anchors, then register under the existing UID lock. It must recheck Control/B3/B5/current heads before/after awaits and at return, with the same single MOVE, call-time burn and shortened expiry. A saved registration never reissues a native owner. Actual database recording/readback timestamps belong to the durable layer; this pure module does not generate them.

SliceB remains separate: the active native population/bundle consumer must stream all required original B1/B3 sources and cross-partition conflicts, use the new abstraction in the same held invocation, preserve the original60s/30s and all resource budgets, and demonstrate an actual original-source33-member continuation. The existing native V1 global32-source bound is untouched by this slice. No source suffix is dropped, no MAX value is raised, and no old receipt is relabelled. Financial finality, funding attribution, loss-streak seed, HWM and eventual clear-stop require their separate producers.

The authored unit cases are declared synthetic original-wire cases only. Until root actually runs matching checks, their status is not executed. PostgreSQL constraints/concurrency, native ownership/TLS, matching CI, Demo and Live remain unaccepted.
