# Demo account completeness-gap inventory

`app/trade_qualification/account_gap_inventory.py` is a **diagnostic-only** view of an existing Demo account capture. It accepts canonical packet bytes plus external plan/packet/input SHA-256 pins. It replays `verify_demo_account_packet` and `materialize_demo_portfolio_snapshot` before listing the capture and mapping reasons still blocking a formal `PortfolioRiskSnapshot`.

The report includes only source digests, gap codes, and the type of source-bound proof still needed. It excludes raw rows, account UID, credential material, and HTTP headers. `recorded_in=capture` means the packet itself reports the gap; `recorded_in=materialization` means the mapping added it. An unfamiliar reason remains visible with `unclassified_source_bound_proof_requires_review`; it cannot disappear through a missing catalog entry. A change to the unconditional capture gaps requires updating the explicit proof catalog.

The inventory enforces the current materializer's fail-closed boundary: packet gaps must remain in the mapping result, the mapping must be incomplete, and no formal snapshot may have been issued. It reports `account_complete=false`, `source_authenticity_verified=false`, and `execution_authority=false`. Its frozen bytes and digest permit readback, but they are **not** an account-completeness receipt or an order authorization. `verify_demo_account_gap_inventory` replays the pinned inputs and rejects even a freshly rehashed edited report.

Examples of outstanding proof classes are an owned authenticated session bound to the exact UID, cross-source account revision and reconciliation, durable history watermarks and retained archives, local in-flight/uncertain/reservation readback, source-bound realized-outcome seed and peak window, active protection, and complete instrument/cost mapping. These are requirements to establish from independent sources; a packet or a locally supplied supplemental input does not itself satisfy them. The current capture intentionally retains its base gaps, so this inventory cannot make R7 account data complete or permit trading.

The focused regression uses synthetic rows only: `tests/unit/test_qualification_account_gap_inventory.py`. It checks actual capture versus materializer findings, pin enforcement, deterministic replay, and rejection of an edited report. No credentials, private network calls, exchange orders, database mutation, or Docker operation are involved.
