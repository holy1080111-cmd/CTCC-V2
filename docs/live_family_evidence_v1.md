# Live family evidence

The closed Live evidence profile now has an explicit
`LiveFamilyEvidenceSnapshotV1`. It retains the exact
`OwnedFamilyEntryQualificationResultV2`, including original family policy and
history admission digests. The four history-dependent strategy families share
the original structural replay and fixed candidate geometry with the Demo
evidence implementation; the four base families retain their base result type.

Live and Demo keep different quote types, validators and wire schemas. Neither
profile accepts the other profile's snapshot. Reopening a report or publishing
six files creates no qualification, account source or trading authority.
Publication still requires new immutable files and complete readback before its
measured barrier; an existing receipt cannot receive a new barrier.

the original Demo codecs, Live base codecs, and both directions of all four
history-dependent families. The added fixtures are explicitly synthetic
artifact consistency tests. They do not establish Live financial completeness,
