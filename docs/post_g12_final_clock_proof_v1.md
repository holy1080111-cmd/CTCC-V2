# Post-G12 final clock proof v1

The V2 public/account join, V3 history join, V4 event observation, and V5 local portfolio observation remain **DENY-only diagnostics**. Their new `clock1` receipt schemas embed a versioned `final_clock_proof`; old receipt schemas remain readable exactly as written and cannot be rewritten or converted into authority.

The proof stores the pinned public journal SHA-256, its persisted `summary.completed` native UTC/monotonic pair, the final native pair, and the effective UTC cutoff. V2/V3 also store the native sample taken when their public capture was consumed. Their `clock1` receipts pin the original candidate deadline; earlier receipt schemas have no such field and remain DENY-only. The cutoff is the maximum of the final UTC sample and every elapsed-monotonic floor from these anchors. This can only shorten the usable lifetime of the original candidate and public quote. The final UTC sample remains unmodified.

Before publishing V2/V3, the producer reopens and replays the raw public journal, checks its summary and packet hashes, compares its native completion pair, and checks root identity. V4/V5 independently reopen the pinned public journal at their later reads. Each receipt readback checks exact schema, canonical bytes, the pinned journal hash, clock sequence validity, recomputed cutoff, and DENY-only fields. For an advanced diagnostic code, it also checks the effective cutoff precedes the original candidate deadline. V4 additionally binds its proof cutoff to the separately pinned DB observation's `received_at`; a proof that predates that observation is rejected on sidecar readback. A standalone receipt read without its public root can replay the arithmetic and hash binding but cannot independently establish that the embedded public pair came from the source journal; source-backed acceptance must reopen the pinned journal.

Invalid, missing, reversed, overlong, or inconsistent pairs stay denied. A clock proof does not authenticate host time, account data, source origin, original G1–G11, risk, reservation, intent, or an order. None of these receipts grants execution eligibility, and no old receipt gains a new qualification.
