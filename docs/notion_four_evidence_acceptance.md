# Original Notion four-evidence acceptance

The original [CTCC remote-control construction instruction](https://app.notion.com/p/3d832165a6888173bfb1df896604fc7c), section V, calls for four reproducible artifacts:

1. Full evidence for an old-style high-scoring but incorrect signal that was blocked.
2. Full evidence for a Demo candidate that passed the new qualification flow.
3. A case cancelled by Execution Recheck because price changed while evidence was generated.
4. A `summary.png` visibly marking Entry, Timing, SL, TP, liquidity and Gate results.

These are the original four items. Later status-page categories do not replace them. The [implementation plan](https://app.notion.com/p/3d832165a68881b3981cd9692986f031) item 21 was unchecked when read on 2026-10-13 and recorded 0/4 real examples; synthetic fixtures do not count. Items 19–20 for real Shadow/Demo were also unchecked. Keep these states until source-backed examples are produced and independently read back. The [single-trade report database](https://app.notion.com/p/13d5e61fce534184a42e0b01c4f372d3) is an outcome log, not the checklist for these four artifacts.
