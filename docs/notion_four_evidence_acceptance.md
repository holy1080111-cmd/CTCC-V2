1. Full evidence for an old-style high-scoring but incorrect signal that was blocked.
2. Full evidence for a Demo candidate that passed the new qualification flow.
3. A case cancelled by Execution Recheck because price changed while evidence was generated.
4. A `summary.png` visibly marking Entry, Timing, SL, TP, liquidity and Gate results.

These are the original four items. Later status-page categories do not replace them.
