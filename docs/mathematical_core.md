# Causal mathematical fusion core

The mathematical core joins analysis evidence into one read-only, auditable
contract. It cannot place an order, add strategy points, move protection, arm
automation, or enable Demo or Live writes.

## Inputs and causality

Only confirmed past candles from 4H, 1H, 15m, and 5m are accepted. The
still-forming candle is removed before every calculation. The core combines:

| Family | Validation treatment | Execution weight |
|---|---|---:|
| Structure | Uncalibrated qualitative evidence; auxiliary only | 0% |
| Momentum | Uncalibrated heuristic evidence; auxiliary only | 0% |
| Local derivative | Analytically checked causal calculation | 1/3 |
| Robust state | Analytically checked state-space calculation | 1/3 |
| Conformal interval | Prequential coverage must pass | 1/3 |

The timeframe weights are 35% for 4H, 30% for 1H, 25% for 15m, and 10% for
5m. Missing timeframes reduce coverage rather than being silently
renormalized to full confidence.

Execution families use equal priors because no retained out-of-sample evidence
currently proves that one fixed family weight is optimal. Their calculated
reliability still adapts the effective weight at every run. Auxiliary structure
and momentum likewise use equal priors inside their isolated tie-break score.

## Validation separation

Every component is labelled `analytical`, `prequential`, or `auxiliary`.
Analytical means its causal formula, geometry, finite-number handling, and
numerical invariants are checked; it does not mean profitable prediction.
Prequential means the outcome at each validation step was not used to form its
own threshold. Structure and momentum remain auxiliary until retained outcome
data supports a separate out-of-sample validation.

For a conformal component to enter the execution core, every available
timeframe needs at least 30 prequential coverage observations and its nominal
coverage must lie inside the two-sided 95% Wilson interval around observed
coverage:

```text
center = (p_hat + z^2 / (2n)) / (1 + z^2 / n)
half   = z * sqrt(p_hat*(1-p_hat)/n + z^2/(4n^2)) / (1 + z^2/n)
pass   = nominal_coverage in [center - half, center + half]
```

This is a coverage diagnostic, not a claim that dependent market outcomes are
independent Bernoulli trials or that the interval predicts profit. A failed or
undersized conformal check is demoted to auxiliary evidence.

## Robust state estimate

The latest 34 confirmed closes form a constant-acceleration state:

```text
x = [log_price, velocity, acceleration]
x[t] = F*x[t-1] + process_noise
y[t] = H*x[t] + observation_noise
```

Return median absolute deviation and residual RMS determine a bounded local
noise scale. A Huber innovation weight inflates observation variance when the
standardized innovation exceeds 2.5. This limits the influence of an isolated
wick while retaining a `shock_score` for the safety layer. The output includes
velocity and acceleration model-based standard errors, z-scores, the latest
innovation, outlier count, a 95% direction interval, and bounded confidence.
These uncertainty values are conditional on the local state-space noise model;
they are not exchange-wide posterior probabilities.

## Causal conformal interval

The latest 60 one-bar residuals are calibrated sequentially. Every residual is
generated by a 21-candle trend fit ending before the observed target candle.
For confidence level `p=0.90`, the finite-sample residual rank is:

```text
rank = ceil((n + 1) * p)
interval = predicted_log_return +/- residual[rank]
```

An interval entirely above zero is rising, an interval entirely below zero is
falling, and an interval crossing zero is uncertain. This is uncertainty
evidence, not a profit or coverage guarantee under arbitrary market drift.
The reported empirical coverage is prequential: after the first 20 residuals,
each outcome is checked against a threshold formed only from still-earlier
residuals. It is therefore not the optimistic in-sample hit rate obtained by
checking calibration observations against their own final quantile.
Residuals below `1e-24` log-return units are treated as Decimal arithmetic
noise; this floor is far below exchange price precision and the API's
12-decimal analytical serialization.

## Fusion equations

Each execution-eligible family produces a directional signal `s[k]` in
`[-1, 1]` and reliability `r[k]` in `[0, 1]`. With execution weight `a[k]`:

```text
directional_score = sum(a[k] * r[k] * s[k]) / sum(a[k] * r[k])
coverage          = sum(a[k] * r[k])
consensus         = 1 - weighted_absolute_disagreement / 2
confidence        = coverage * consensus * abs(directional_score)
                    * (1 - instability)
```

`confidence` is a bounded decision statistic, not a probability of profit or
a calibrated forecast probability. The family weights are explicit risk
weights; the fusion does not assume that derivative, state, and conformal
signals are statistically independent merely because all three are present.

Auxiliary structure and momentum are fused separately. A conformal component
that fails validation keeps its auxiliary audit label but receives zero
execution and bonus weight; failing a check can never improve priority.
Aligned auxiliary confidence is converted to an integer audit bonus from 0 to
5. It is evaluated only after execution score, raw score, and validated
confidence are tied. It cannot change eligibility, `score`, `risk_score`,
leverage, margin, stop risk, or an execution blocker.

```text
auxiliary_bonus = floor(5 * auxiliary_confidence) if aligned else 0
```

Instability includes robust state shocks, extreme volatility, and failed data
quality. The core reports `long`, `short`, `neutral`, `unstable`, or
`insufficient`. A direction-specific candidate then receives one risk grade:

| Mathematical result | Risk grade | Demo effect when adaptive sizing is enabled |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed, confidence/reliability at least 0.65, instability at most 0.20 | High | Raw score may be retained |
| Confirmed, confidence at least 0.35 and reliability at least 0.45 | Medium | Effective score capped below high tier |
| Mixed, insufficient, or weaker confirmation | Low | Effective score capped below medium tier |
| Opposed or unstable | Blocked | Candidate vetoed before leverage or order write |

The mapping is monotonic: mathematical evidence can only retain, cap, or block
the raw strategy score. The risk engine evaluates `risk_score` when present,
so downstream sizing cannot accidentally fall back to a higher raw score.
When several strategies are otherwise eligible, selection compares their
downward-capped mathematical score before the raw score; a weakly confirmed
95-point candidate therefore cannot displace a strongly confirmed 90-point
candidate merely because its pre-mathematical score was larger.

## Deliberate exclusions

Cross-instrument covariance shrinkage and portfolio CVaR optimization are not
claimed in this gate. They require synchronized, retained multi-instrument
return scenarios and separate out-of-sample calibration. Treating BTC and ETH
as statistically independent without that evidence would create false
precision. The existing aggregate stop-risk, margin, notional, position-count,
available-equity, and exchange-rounding ceilings remain authoritative.

Bayesian online change-point inference is also not claimed. This gate uses the
robust state's causal shock score as the conservative transition veto. A full
run-length posterior needs its own calibration and acceptance evidence.
